About Us

Vasquez And Vasquez Painting

Our Story

Vasquez & Vasquez Painting is a family-owned company with over 25 years of experience, built on trust, quality, and attention to detail.

We believe every home deserves to look and feel better, regardless of size or budget. That's why we offer honest, flexible, and personalized solutions for every client.

We specialize in residential and commercial painting, interior and exterior, using high-quality materials and focusing on proper preparation for long-lasting results.

More than a service, we provide a reliable, clean, and family-style experience.

Because to us, it's not just paint — it's your home.

Professional

CUSTOM PAINTING

At Vasquez and Vasquez Painting, we specialize in transforming homes with precision and care. Our professional remodeling services include everything from kitchen cabinet refinishing to stair and interior upgrades. With a focus on quality craftsmanship and attention to detail, we bring your vision to life — creating elegant, modern spaces that reflect your style and enhance your home's value.

Meet Our

Team

Michaell Vasquez Co-Founder & Painter

Born in Miami and raised in Colombia, Michaell found his passion for painting in Tampa, FL. Inspired by his father's legacy, he co-founded Vasquez & Vasquez Painting in 2014, dedicated to transforming spaces and exceeding every client's expectations.

Victor Vasquez Co-Founder & Master Craftsman

With decades of experience in Colombia and Miami, Victor specializes in textures, Venetian plaster, and stain applications. His eye for design and craftsmanship is the backbone of every project Vasquez & Vasquez undertakes.

Angelica Guzman Operations & Marketing

A Colombian entrepreneur with a strong background in logistics and business development, Angelica drives the company's operations, team coordination, and marketing efforts — bringing structure and vision to every step of the business.
