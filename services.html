Our Services

Professional Painting and Home Improvement in St. Petersburg

Based in St. Petersburg, Vasquez & Vasquez Painting provides residential and light commercial painting, surface preparation, and home improvement services. We help local property owners with interior and exterior finishes, repairs, and upgrades, and we can confirm availability for nearby Tampa Bay communities when you request an estimate.

Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Vasquez & Vasquez Painting offer?

We provide interior and exterior painting, cabinet painting, staining, drywall and texture repair, power washing, flooring, carpentry, stucco, siding, and related home improvement services.

Do you work on residential and commercial properties?

Yes. We serve residential properties and light commercial projects. Contact us with the project details so we can confirm the scope and availability.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. You can request a free estimate through our website or call us at 813-847-7863.

Is surface preparation included in a painting project?

Project preparation can include cleaning, patching, sanding, caulking, minor repairs, and protecting surrounding areas. The exact preparation is reviewed during the estimate.

How long will my painting project take?

Timing depends on the size, surface condition, preparation, weather, and selected service. We provide a project-specific timeline with the estimate.

What area do you serve?

We are based in St. Petersburg, Florida. Contact us to confirm availability for your address in a nearby Tampa Bay community.

Do you move and protect furniture before painting?

The estimate identifies what the customer should move and what the crew will protect. Small and fragile items should be removed before work begins; larger furniture, floors, and nearby surfaces are covered or relocated as agreed for the project.

Can you work in occupied homes or businesses?

Many projects can be scheduled in occupied homes and light commercial properties. We review work areas, access, ventilation, drying time, and daily cleanup before starting. Availability depends on the project scope and site conditions.

What paint brands and finishes do you use?

Products are selected for the surface, existing coating, interior or exterior exposure, desired appearance, durability, and budget. The proposed brand, product, and sheen are confirmed for each project during the estimate.
